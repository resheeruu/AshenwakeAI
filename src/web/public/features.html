Features

AI chat, moderation, automation, personality, social features, and multi-provider AI in one Discord-native platform.

AI Chat

Conversational AI with multi-provider routing and fallback. Supports Google Gemini, Groq, OpenRouter, and local models.

Multi-provider AI

Access dozens of AI providers through a single interface. Free-tier, trial, and paid options available.

Custom Personality

Configure AI tone, style, and behavior for your server. Set name, humor, creativity, and verbosity levels.

AI Moderation

Spam detection, link filtering, mention abuse, and toxicity prevention. Configurable actions: warn, delete, timeout.

Automation

Welcome messages, role assignment, and event triggers. Event/condition/action interface with allowlisted actions.

Social/Debate

Rivalry sessions and configurable social behavior. Debates with intensity, cooldown, and maximum session length.

Anime Actions

Custom emoji provisioning and anime-style interactions. Provision and manage custom Discord emoji.

Analytics

AI request tracking, provider health, and usage metrics. Today/7 days/30 day views with lightweight charts.

Server Controls

Channel configuration, role management, and permissions. AI-enabled channels, ignored channels, moderator roles.
